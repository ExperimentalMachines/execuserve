TITLE
ExecuServe: your phone is an OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible AI server

DESCRIPTION
Keep your SDK. Point it at your phone, and it talks to a model running there.

ExecuServe is a free, open-source Android app that keeps ExecuTorch language models loaded on the phone and serves them over HTTP, with the OpenAI and Anthropic APIs your tools already speak.

• OpenAI Chat Completions, Responses and Completions, and Anthropic Messages, with streaming and tool calls
• Our compatibility suites, run with the official SDKs against the release build on a phone: OpenAI 16 of 16, Anthropic 8 of 8, edge cases 28 of 28
• Follow-ups reuse the server's KV cache: on a 2,195-token conversation, the follow-up's first token came 39× sooner than the cold first turn (0.50 s against 19.38 s, 2,202 tokens reused; Qwen3 1.7B on a POCO X8 Pro Max)
• Up to three models resident, sharing one compute lane
• API keys by default, a foreground service that kept serving with the screen off (20 of 20 requests in a 10-minute battery soak), and a browser chat served by the phone itself

Everything in the video is real: the tokens are a recorded reply from Qwen3 1.7B on a POCO X8 Pro Max through the OpenAI SDK, replayed at the moment each arrived, and the browser chat is that phone's own page.
