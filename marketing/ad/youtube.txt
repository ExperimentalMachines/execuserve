TITLE
ExecuServe: turn your Android phone into an AI server

DESCRIPTION
Your phone runs the AI. Your other devices use it.

ExecuServe is a free, open-source Android app that keeps an AI model loaded on your phone and serves it to your laptop, your tablet and your own apps over Wi-Fi.

• Pick a model from the built-in catalog and download it on the phone
• Tap Start: the phone becomes a server, and keeps answering in the background, even with the screen off
• Open the phone's chat page from any browser on your network, with nothing to install
• Point your own code at it: it is compatible with OpenAI and Anthropic clients
• No cloud inference, no account, no subscription

Everything in the video is real: the app's own screen recordings, and a laptop talking to a POCO X8 Pro Max running Qwen3 1.7B. The screen-off answer was recorded with the phone locked and dozing. Downloads and loading are sped up and say so; the answers play at the speed they arrived.
