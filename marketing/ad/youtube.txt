TITLE
ExecuServe: an AI server for any Android phone, on the chip it has

DESCRIPTION
Your phone has a chip built for AI. ExecuServe puts it to work.

ExecuServe is a free, open-source Android app that runs an AI model on your phone's own processor and serves it to the other apps on the phone and every device on your network.

• Runs on the chip you have: the CPU on any Android phone, the GPU through Vulkan, and the NPU on Snapdragon (Qualcomm QNN) and MediaTek Dimensity (NeuroPilot) phones. The app offers your phone the builds compiled for its own chip.
• On a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, the NPU read a 700-token prompt in 0.41 s, against 1.0 s on the GPU and 2.0 s on the CPU (Qwen3-1.7B, measured with ExecuServe).
• Tap Start: the phone becomes a server and keeps answering in the background, even with the screen off
• Compatible with OpenAI and Anthropic clients, LangChain, the Vercel AI SDK and Open WebUI, or open the phone's chat page from any browser on your network
• No cloud, no account, no subscription

Everything in the video is real: the phones' own screen recordings (a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 on Qualcomm Device Cloud and a POCO X8 Pro Max with a MediaTek Dimensity), and another device asking the POCO over Wi-Fi. The screen-off answer was recorded with the phone locked and dozing. Loading is sped up and says so; the answers play at the speed they arrived.
